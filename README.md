My name is Jennifer Noinaj (noy-NAH), and I go by Jenn. I'm located in Chicago! I'm a civic tech leader working at the intersection of strategy, product, and delivery. My north star has been about creating a government that’s easy to interact with and allows people to get the services they need.

I’m also big on shaping the culture behind work and a firm believer that healthy teams make better products. I help teams evolve from the 'waterfall' mindset to agile, cross-functional collaboration. The best outcomes happen when we break down organizational silos and co-create across design, product, engineering, and policy. Whether it's up-skilling staff or maturing product practices, I love building the "machine that builds the product." 

🎯 **What I Do**
* **Digital Service Delivery:** Partnering with complex public programs to transform critical services into seamless, human-centered digital experiences.
* **Policy to Execution:** Translating complex policy mandates into clear product strategies, actionable roadmaps, and scalable execution.
* **Cross-Functional Leadership:** Aligning diverse stakeholder visions and building high-trust, collaborative environments where cross-functional teams thrive.

🛠 **My Toolkit**
* **Strategy & Leadership:** Product Strategy & Prioritization, Change Enablement, Stakeholder Alignment
* **Research & Service Design:** Journey Mapping, Service Blueprinting, Qualitative & Quantitative Discovery
* **Execution & Delivery:** Agile Methodologies, Mentoring and Coaching, Scalable Product Delivery
